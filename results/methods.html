Adapterwikipedia-scalar-affine.safetensors from keenanpepper/selfie-adapters-llama-3.1-8b-instruct
Adapter formf(h) = α·(h/‖h‖) + b, α = 7.175, ‖b‖ = 20.869, normalize_input=true
Embedderthenlper/gte-large — the paper's choice
Stacktorch 2.8.0+cu128, transformers 5.15.0, sentence-transformers 5.7.0
Samplingtemperature 0.5, 40 new tokens, 6 generations per vector

No adapter was trained. The released checkpoints are used as-is, so this reproduces the paper's artifact, not its training run.

Extraction convention, kept identical to upstream so numbers stay comparable. Topic activations h: "Tell me about {title}." through the chat template with add_generation_prompt=True, last token. Contrastive vector: h − mean_vector[19] — this is what the adapter consumes. Concept activations (extension only): full user+assistant turn, mean-pooled over the response span only, located by character offsets so instruction tokens are excluded.

One code change was needed for transformers 5.x: from_pretrained(torch_dtype=) became dtype=. Handled by signature inspection so the same code runs on both.

SECTION 02Datasets

keenanpepper/fifty-thousand-things — 49,637 Wikipedia vital-article topics with 6–20 alternate descriptions each, split 44,673 / 4,964. Public, 55 MB. Supplies both the reproduction targets and the base activations h. Retrieval document per topic = title + first 8 labels.

pyvene/axbench-concept500 (CC-BY-4.0) — not used by the paper; introduced here to supply concept directions. Four partitions of 500 concepts each, drawn from GemmaScope SAE auto-interp labels. Per concept: 72 positives in train, plus 36 positives and 36 concept-specific negatives in test.

Adapters — four released 19 kB .safetensors, public, no auth.

SECTION 03Failure modes found in the data

All found before GPU time was spent, except F7 — which needed it, and which changed the picture most.

F1 · blocking

load_dataset cannot read AxBench at all

train has 7 columns, test has 9 (sae_link, sae_id extra), so HuggingFace datasets raises CastError: column names don't match.

Fix: read the eight parquet files directly with pandas, per partition.

F2 · opportunity

The pool is 2,000 concepts, not 500

The four partitions are near-disjoint — pairwise label overlap of 0–1 concepts, 1,995 distinct labels across 2,000 rows.

Action: pool all four, giving 108 positives + 36 negatives per concept.

F3 · would have faked the result

Concept-independent style signature in every positive

AxBench positives are LLM-generated to express a concept; negatives are ordinary responses. A classifier trained on some concepts and tested on held-out ones still separates them at 0.969 ± 0.005 AUC — on style alone. "akin": 17.2% of positives vs 0.0% of negatives. "just": 15.8% vs 0.6%.

A naive mean_pos − mean_neg vector would encode this register, not the concept — and because style is a real, consistent direction, it would pass a norm-matched random-direction control. That is a false positive for the paper's faithfulness, manufactured by an artifact of the steering data.

Fix: cross-concept mean-centring, plus a dedicated style-direction control arm in every experiment.

F4 · unfixable, scoped

Instruction pools are disjoint and concept-matched

Instructions alone separate positives from negatives at 0.929 AUC, and the two instruction pools overlap 0% within a concept. Worse, instructions are concept-matched — a concept's instructions predict its own label at 35.8% top-50 versus ~0.2% chance — so mean-centring cannot remove this.

Judgement: less dangerous than F3, since instruction topic points in the same semantic direction as the label. Recorded as a scope limit: v_c blends "concept in the response" with "topic of its associated prompts."

F5 · a fix that does not work

Length imbalance, and why truncation doesn't help

Positives run 46% longer (501 vs 344 chars; length alone gives 0.704 AUC). Truncating everything to the negative median leaves style separability essentially unchanged — 0.966 vs 0.969.

Action: length-matching was dropped from the plan. It buys nothing here; centring has to do the work.

F6 · clean

Genre is balanced

Text / code / math proportions are identical between positives and negatives (0.6767 / 0.2500 / 0.0733 each). No genre confound.

F7 · the proxy lied

In activation space the confound is 4× worse than any text check suggests

Every check above is TF-IDF. Measured on real Llama layer-19 activations, the shared component is nearly the entire vector:

Corrections applied

  • Cross-concept mean-centring — v_c ← v_c − mean(v), the same operation the paper applies to topic vectors. Result: mean pairwise cosine −0.0132 against the mechanical floor of −0.0169, i.e. as orthogonal as centring permits.
  • Positives-only construction rather than pos − neg. Statistically equivalent after centring, but never differences two disjoint instruction pools (F4), and mirrors the paper's own topic-vector construction.
  • Split-half stability — vectors rebuilt from alternating halves. Median cosine 0.939, min 0.826, 100% above 0.5.

SECTION 05The extension, experiment by experiment

None of the following appear in the paper. Each was added to test whether descriptions respond to a controlled change, rather than merely correlating with an activation.

5.1 · Adapter geometry in closed form (no GPU)

Why: the paper reports the bias accounts for ~85% of the improvement but never characterises the geometry that implies. How: normalize_input=true means the instance term has fixed norm, so every soft token lies on a sphere of radius α centred at b — computable from the 19 kB checkpoint alone.

goodfire-sae4.03219.7504.0%23.6°0.959980.96000
llamascope-sae4.02119.0084.3%24.4°0.957180.95717

Meaning: every one of ~50,000 interpretations launches from inside a single 40° cone. But this bounds the instance budget, not its usage — §4 shows the model resolves 1-in-49,637 inside that cone. Geometry alone cannot settle the question, which is why the sweep is needed. It also makes a falsifiable prediction: a model this sensitive to small angular displacement should register a steering vector, so a flat curve would be the surprise.

5.2 · Bias-only retrieval

Why: the paper notes a zero vector yields generic descriptions but never scores them. Result: 0.0% R@1, while producing fluent, confident, specific text — "the director of the 1967 film Blow-Up", "the journalist who coined the term 'tropism'".

Meaning: the sharpest qualification of the paper's own framing. "The bias accounts for ~85% of the improvement" is fully compatible with the bias carrying zero instance information. It supplies format, register and confident fluency; the instance term does all the identifying. It also shows the prior is an active confabulator rather than a neutral scaffold — directly relevant to the deceptive-alignment motivation.

5.3 · Behavioural validation, doubling as concept screening

Why: without independent evidence the intervention changes behaviour, the sweep is pure semantic correlation. How: a forward hook adds λ·‖resid‖·v̂ at layer 19 across all positions during generation (CAA-style), 20 neutral prompts, same λ grid as the sweep, λ in units of typical residual norm so both curves share an x-axis.

5.4 · The λ-sweep, four arms

10 screened concepts × 30 held-out topics × 11 λ × 6 generations. Injection scale held fixed across λ so the paper's best-of-n scale search cannot be mistaken for the λ effect. Scored as rank against a fixed 40-distractor pool, not raw cosine — cosine drifts upward whenever text merely gets longer or more generic, which would read as a false positive.

Each arm isolates something different. random — direction-specificity versus a generic response to perturbation; the control that could have killed the result. style — response to any real consistent direction the model represents, which a random direction cannot test, and which exists only because F3 was found. pure_v (λ‖h‖v̂ with no h) — degeneracy: wherever concept ≈ pure_v, h no longer matters.

Meaning: S(+λ) > S(0) > S(−λ), monotone and bidirectional. Both controls flat. pure_v saturates at λ=0.3 while concept climbs gradually — at λ=0.3 concept 0.558 ≪ pure_v 0.796, so h still dominates; by λ=2 they converge and h is washed out. The informative regime is λ ≲ 1. Because the adapter L2-normalises, adding λv can only rotate direction, never change magnitude — no norm confound can explain any of it.

5.6 · Variance decomposition

Description embeddings are unit-norm, so the second moment splits exactly as 1 = ‖ē‖² + Var; the variance term is then partitioned factorially. Validated beforehand against synthetic data with closed-form ground truth, including a null case that must not fire (predicted prior share 0.9493 vs measured 0.9553; null-case λ share 0.005).

SECTION 06Implementation

~2,000 lines across 12 modules. Nothing upstream is vendored — it was cloned for reference, and the pieces needed (adapter format, injection template, extraction convention) reimplemented so the pipeline runs without its GPU-heavy dependency set (sae-lens, nnsight, vllm).

Choices worth naming

  • Adapter reads the released safetensors directly — tensors bias (4096,) and log_scale (1,) — rather than importing selfie_adapters. Drops a dependency, and lets random_like() fabricate an untrained adapter at the measured geometry for smoke runs on a model with different d.
  • Model-agnostic SelfIE template. Upstream hard-codes Llama's <|reserved_special_token_0|>; ours derives an equivalent prompt from any tokenizer's chat template. This is what makes the CPU smoke path possible at all.
  • Batched selfie_describe. The first version issued one generate() per vector; flattening (B, n) into one batched call is why 79,206 generations finish in under two hours.
  • Steering as a context-managed forward hook, handling both tuple and tensor block outputs, handle removed in a finally. The smoke test explicitly asserts the hook is removed cleanly and that coeff=0 is a true no-op — both silent-corruption failure modes.

Two local environments

The 2019 Intel Mac has no CUDA and no MPS, and PyTorch ships no macOS x86_64 wheel past 2.2.x (cp311 max). So: .venv (Python 3.13, no torch) ran all of Phase 0 and the entire dataset audit — safetensors.numpy.load_file reads checkpoints without torch at all. .venv311 (brew install python@3.11, torch==2.2.2, numpy<2 since the 2.2.2 binary is built against numpy 1.x) ran CPU smoke tests.

Every GPU script takes --smoke, swapping in Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct with a random adapter on CPU. Outputs are nonsense; the point is validating shapes, hook placement and templates before spending GPU time. The full chain ran end-to-end this way locally first.

SECTION 07How it ran on RunPod

Provisioned through the RunPod MCP server rather than the console. Selection was driven by a constraint that only appears if you check: no 48GB GPU has a network volume in its region. A40 exists only in CA-MTL-1 and EU-SE-1, neither volume-capable; A6000 likewise; L40S has one volume-capable region at 2–3× the price and LOW stock. The only HIGH-stock GPU in a volume-capable region is the 24GB RTX 4090.

Chosen: A40 48GB, CA-MTL-1, Secure Cloud, $0.44/hr, no network volume — VRAM headroom over persistence, since re-setup is ~15 minutes and scriptable while a 24GB ceiling is permanent.

create-pod  name=selfie-adapter-probes  gpuTypeIds=["NVIDIA A40"]
            dataCenterIds=["CA-MTL-1"]  cloudType=SECURE
            containerDiskInGb=20  volumeInGb=100  volumeMountPath=/workspace
            ports=["22/tcp","8888/http"]  sshPublicKey=<ed25519 public key>
            env={HF_HOME:/workspace/hf_cache}

The HF token was supplied as a RunPod Secret, referenced as HF_TOKEN={{ RUNPOD_SECRET_hf_token }}, so it never entered the session transcript; the account-scoped OAuth token on the laptop was deliberately not copied to rented hardware. Code moved by rsync over SSH, excluding venvs, .git, and the 73MB pair table that regenerates from HuggingFace in a minute.

Everything ran through one detached orchestration script:

setsid nohup env N_EVAL=1000 N_CONCEPTS=60 SWEEP_CONCEPTS=10 SWEEP_TOPICS=30 \
  bash run_all.sh > /workspace/pipeline.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &

It appends a marker to /workspace/PIPELINE_STEPS after each phase (REPRO_OK, …, PIPELINE_COMPLETE, or FAILED:<phase>), so progress is a one-line SSH poll rather than a held-open connection. 07:52 → 09:37 UTC, ≈$0.80 for all 79,206 generations.

Six things that cost real time

  • update-pod replaces the env wholesale — it does not merge. Setting HF_TOKEN while omitting PUBLIC_KEY deleted the variable the image uses to install the SSH key and start sshd. Instant lockout, two restarts to recover.
  • Pod env vars are absent from SSH sessions. They live on PID 1. echo $HF_TOKEN returned empty and looked exactly like an unresolved secret — it had resolved correctly all along. Read them from /proc/1/environ.
  • The public SSH port changes on every restart (22104 → 22019 → 22015 → 22179).
  • Container disk resets on restart; only /workspace persists.
  • /workspace is MooseFS network storage — 453 MB/s sequential, fine for a 16GB model, but very slow on many-small-file work. pip install there took ~20 minutes and twice appeared to hang. PEP 668 also blocks system-wide pip on this image, so a venv is mandatory.
  • rsync -a fails on /workspace with chown: Operation not permitted. Use scp or rsync -rlptz --no-owner --no-group.

For any long pod job: setsid nohup … & plus a completion-marker file. Plain nohup over SSH dies when the session drops — that is how the first two installs were lost.

SECTION 08Limitations

  • Not causal. Steering vectors are not clean semantic representations. This tests sensitivity to a controlled, independently validated change — nothing more.
  • Selection effect, twice over. Concepts were filtered for lexical separability and chosen because they steer well and preserve fluency. Results describe the favourable case.
  • Weak concepts in the swept set. Only 5 of the 10 swept concepts had behavioural rise > 0.3 (median 0.377), and the two weakest (0.240, 0.219) are not far above the style control's 0.153. The fluency filter bound harder than the steering-strength filter, so the final set trades effect size for coherence. All 10 did have high monotonicity (0.86–0.98).
  • Behavioural control arms are not absolutely comparable. __STYLE__ and __RANDOM__ were scored against a placeholder label, so their absolute rank values mean nothing; only their rise (0.153 and −0.049 versus up to 0.945) is interpretable.
  • Topic displacement is partly generic — T(λ) also declines for random and style.
  • Instruction confound persists (F4) and cannot be removed by centring.
  • Narrow. One model, one layer, one adapter architecture, 10 concepts, 30 topics. Reproduction is partial by design.

What would strengthen this next

In rough order of value: an instruction-only control arm to bound F4 empirically; the same sweep on wikipedia-full-rank and the SAE adapters, whose instance share is less than half; more concepts, to turn the 0.75% variance figure into something with an interval; and the genuinely stronger test — intervening on features or circuits whose causal role is independently established, and asking whether the self-report tracks that.
